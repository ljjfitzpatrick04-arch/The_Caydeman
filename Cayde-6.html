Some Facts

In the world of Destiny. Cayde-6 who is voiced by the Great Nathan fillian. is a character that is loved my pretty much everybody. He is often like an older brother. He is there for you when it matters most, but he keeps a calm and cool head while always making the mood of the situation much lighter. in his eyes, while everyone else is nose deep in drama, and dark moments. there needs to be someone to break the ice, and give people that feeling of hope. honestly; how can you feel that all hope is lost, when Cayde is Cracking jokes? even when staring right at death, he never lost that edge. It wasnt until He finally met his end, at the Hands of Uldren Sov where we lost that bright spark of hope. and it was cayde-6 sacrafice, which ultimately led to us Defeating the Witness. Without cayde, none would have been posible.

Cayde Got his Title after his mentor Andal Brask died to the hands of Tankis, a fallen Captain at the time. through the art of "The Hunter Dare" see, you would think that its a priveledge to be the Hunter Vanguard, and gettin to rule over the Tower. But to a hunter, the title is death. hunters are made to be free, and be out in the wild, not stuck in the tower, behind a desk, giving Orders so that other hunters can be free. so as a Curse, hunters came up with a thing known as the Hunter Dare which goes as follows. "If you kill me; you get all of my possesions, Titles, ranks, Status, fame." basically, When you fight the Hunter Vanguard, you are taking on a challenge. but you lose that challenge if you defeat him. Andal Braks had Dared Cayde-6 when they were hunting down Taniks. if Andal Dies, cayde takes up the mantle.

What is the Reason for the "6" in Caydes name you ask? its because he is an exo. and exo's are (used to be human) but have had their conciousness converted and placed insdie a metal electrical Body. the Number in an Exo's name, represents the Number of times that Exo has been rebooted. so, Banshee-44, has had his memeory wiped and rebooted 44 times. the Reason for rebooting is usually because of Death as an Exo. even though they are Guardians. But for whatever reason sometimes they need to be rebooted. the problem with having been rebooted so many times as in the case with Banshee-44. it actually scrambles alot of the data that makes up that memory of that character. that the initial memory of that person actually starts to get overwritten, and fade. creating a sort of Block in their memory that can screw with their cognitive abilities. Cayde has only been rebooted 6 times. he does have some memory loss, but its not a severe as someone like Banshee-44.

Ace of Spades

Cayde-6 carries with him at all times his trusty Hand Cannon; the Ace of Spades. in the Game; the Player used to have to play a lot of crucible and get lots of Final blows, Specifically with headshots from handcannons to finally obtain this beast. its perks are, with every headshot kill, it causes the target to explode, and reloading after a Kill provides 6 Memento Mori bullets which have increased Damage.

Still Hunt

He also owns a sniper, named Still hunt. which we rarely see him use. in the game; the player gets ahold of this weapon when they complete one of the sidequest missions during the Final Shape Campaign. its perks are that after hitting a few headshots/percision hits, or picking up enough orbs of light. the Gun gets the ability to enter Golden gun mode which provides 3 high-damage Golden gun shots(or 1 shot that counts as 6 if using Celestial Nighthawk as a hunter)

Gunslinger Class

Cayde is a Gunslinger, who uses the Solar Subclass for hunters, and fights with the "Golden Gun" Super. he uses tripmine grenades, and sometimes uses blade Barrage when he really needs it. Cayde-6 is One of, if not the fastest, and most accurate Shots in the vanguard. in his past, he was even able to land a hit on the almighty Shaxx. which is incredibly difficult.

The player Will access to 3 hunter light-subclasses. Gunslinger being the solar option. you get access to many abilities with this subclass. a choice between 3 supers(deadshot Golden Gun, marksman Golden Gun, Bladebarrage). a bunch of different melee(throwing knife) options, Solar Grenades, and 4 different aspects of which they can only choose to equip up to 2 at any time. these abilits define the hunter, and allow the player to customise and build craft the Solar hunter to play in the way they figure is best. in terms of Cayde-6 though, we see that he primarily prefers the Golden gun Super, and we only ever see him use the Tripmine grenade.
